# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## 1. Shipping options and prices
| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3 to 7 business days | $5.99, **free for orders of $50 or more** |
| Express | 1 to 2 business days | $14.99 |
| Bulky item delivery (appliances over 10 kg) | 5 to 10 business days | $24.99, free for orders of $500 or more |

Delivery times start when the order is "confirmed", not when it is placed. Weekends and public holidays are not counted as business days.

## 2. Order processing
Orders are confirmed immediately when stock is available. If a product runs out of stock while the order is being processed, the order is marked "failed" with the reason "out_of_stock" and the customer is not charged.

## 3. Delivery address
- Orders ship to the address entered at checkout, or to the default address saved in the customer profile.
- The address cannot be changed after the order has the status "shipped". Before that, cancel the order and place a new one.
- We ship within the country of the seller's warehouse and to the countries listed on the checkout page.

## 4. Tracking
Once an order is shipped, the order details page shows the carrier and tracking reference. Orders that were confirmed more than 24 hours ago are automatically shown as "delivered" in the current system, which is a simplification used for this platform.

## 5. Missed or delayed deliveries
- Carriers attempt delivery up to 3 times. After that the parcel is returned to the warehouse and the customer receives a refund minus the shipping fee.
- If an order has not arrived 5 business days after the latest estimated delivery date, contact support. A lost parcel is replaced or refunded in full.

## 6. Damaged in transit
Do not accept a parcel with visible damage if the carrier is still present. Otherwise report the damage within 7 days. See the Return and Refund Policy, section 4.
