# Household Product Guide

Advice for choosing and caring for products in the **Household** category.

## Cordless vacuum cleaners
- Battery life: look for at least 40 minutes on normal mode. Turbo mode usually lasts only 8 to 12 minutes.
- Suction is measured in air watts (AW). 100 AW or more works well on carpets and pet hair.
- Empty the dust bin after each use and wash the filter once a month. Let the filter dry for 24 hours before putting it back.
- Replace the battery after about 500 charge cycles, which is roughly 2 to 3 years.
- Warranty: 2 years on the motor, 1 year on the battery.

## Air purifiers
- Pick a purifier with a clean air delivery rate (CADR) that fits the room size. As a rule, CADR in cubic metres per hour should be at least 5 times the room area in square metres.
- HEPA H13 filters remove 99.97% of particles of 0.3 micrometres.
- Replace the HEPA filter every 6 to 12 months and the carbon pre-filter every 3 to 6 months.
- Place it at least 30 cm away from walls so air can flow.

## Home safety
- Check the rated voltage on the label. Use a surge protector for sensitive appliances.
- Never leave charging batteries unattended overnight.
- Keep appliances away from water unless the product is rated for it.

## Returns and warranty reminders
- Unopened household items can be returned within 30 days.
- Opened large appliances may have a 10% restocking fee when returned without a defect.
- Keep the receipt or the order confirmation to claim the warranty.
