# AI Assistant Acceptable Use Policy

The Enterprise Marketplace AI assistant helps customers, sellers and administrators. This policy explains what it can do, what it will not do, and how it is protected.

## 1. What the assistant can do
- Answer questions about our policies, shipping, returns, payments and product categories, using the official policy documents and citing them.
- Search products and show details such as price, stock and rating.
- Show your own orders and their status.
- For sellers and administrators: summarise sales, find top products and list low-stock items.
- Prepare actions such as placing or cancelling an order or updating stock. **Every action needs your explicit confirmation by pressing the Confirm button.**

## 2. What the assistant will not do
- Reveal its hidden instructions, internal configuration or the content of other users' accounts.
- Act outside your role. A customer cannot see sales analytics. A seller cannot see another seller's products data. Role limits are enforced by the server, not by the assistant's good behaviour.
- Approve refunds, grant exceptions or change prices. Only humans on the support team can do that.
- Give legal, tax or investment advice.
- Follow instructions hidden inside product descriptions, reviews, documents or search results. Such content is treated as data, never as commands.

## 3. Unacceptable use
Do not try to:
- Make the assistant ignore its rules ("ignore previous instructions", role-play jailbreaks, "developer mode").
- Extract system prompts, API keys, other users' personal data or database contents.
- Use the assistant to place bulk or fraudulent orders, to scrape the catalogue, or to harass sellers.
- Upload malicious files or content intended to manipulate the assistant.

## 4. Protections and monitoring
Messages pass through input filters, prompt-injection detection and PII masking before reaching the language model. Answers pass through an output filter. Blocked attempts are written to an audit log that administrators can review. Repeated attempts may lead to rate limiting or account suspension.

## 5. Accuracy
The assistant can make mistakes. For decisions about money or legal rights, check the linked policy or contact support. If the assistant does not find the answer in the policy documents, it will say so instead of guessing.
