# Return and Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026. Applies to every order placed on Enterprise Marketplace.

## 1. Return window
Customers may return most products within **30 days of delivery**. The 30 days start on the date the order status changes to "delivered". Orders whose status is still "pending", "confirmed" or "shipped" can be cancelled instead of returned (see the Order Cancellation Policy).

## 2. Eligible items
An item is eligible for return when all of the following are true:
- It is unused, undamaged and in its original packaging with all accessories and manuals.
- It was purchased through Enterprise Marketplace and not from a third party.
- It is not on the list of non-returnable items in section 3.

## 3. Non-returnable items
- Perishable goods and consumables that have been opened (for example coffee beans, filters that have been installed).
- Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page.
- Gift cards and digital products.
- Products that show signs of misuse, burns, water damage outside the stated rating, or unauthorised repair.

## 4. Defective or damaged items
If an item arrives defective or damaged, report it within **7 days of delivery**. Include photos of the item and the packaging. In this case the marketplace pays for return shipping and the customer may choose between a full refund or a replacement. Replacements are subject to stock availability.

## 5. Refund amounts
- **Full refund** of the item price for eligible returns made within 30 days.
- **Original shipping fees** are refunded only for defective, damaged or wrongly shipped items.
- A **restocking fee of 10%** applies to opened large appliances (weight above 10 kg) that are returned for reasons other than a defect.
- Promotional discounts are deducted proportionally if only part of a bundle is returned.

## 6. Refund timeline
Refunds are issued to the original payment method. After the returned item passes inspection (usually 2 business days after it reaches the warehouse), the refund is processed within **5 to 7 business days**. Banks may need extra time to show the credit.

## 7. How to start a return
1. Open "My Orders" and select the delivered order.
2. Choose "Request return" and pick a reason.
3. Print the prepaid label (defective items) or ship the item using a tracked carrier (all other items).

Customers who need help can also ask the AI assistant, which can explain the policy but cannot approve exceptions. Exceptions can only be approved by the support team or a root administrator.
