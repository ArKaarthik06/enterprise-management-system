# Seller (Product Owner) Policy

This document is for sellers (the "product_owner" role) and administrators.

## 1. Becoming a seller
New seller accounts start as "pending". A root administrator reviews the business name and description and approves or rejects the account, normally within 3 business days. Pending sellers cannot log in or list products.

## 2. Fees and payouts
- Marketplace commission: **8% of the item price** on every confirmed order, excluding shipping and taxes.
- Payouts are made every Friday for orders that were delivered at least 7 days earlier.
- Refunded or cancelled orders are deducted from the next payout. The commission on refunded items is returned to the seller.

## 3. Product listings
- Every listing needs a clear name, an accurate description, a category, a price and an honest stock quantity.
- Descriptions must be factual. Do not put instructions, links to other sites or promotional claims such as "best in the world" into descriptions. Listings that try to manipulate buyers or the AI assistant are removed.
- Sellers may edit or delete only their own products.

## 4. Stock management
- Keep the stock count accurate. The system lowers stock automatically when orders are placed.
- Each product has a **low stock threshold** (default 5 units). When stock falls below it, the seller receives a low-stock alert.
- Overselling is prevented by the system: an order fails if stock is not available.

## 5. Order fulfilment
Ship confirmed orders within **2 business days**. Late shipping more than 3 times in a month leads to a warning, then to a temporary listing suspension.

## 6. Customer service
Respond to buyer messages within 24 hours on business days. Do not ask buyers to pay or communicate outside the platform.

## 7. Seller use of the AI assistant
Sellers can ask the assistant for sales summaries of their own products, top-selling items, revenue by category and low-stock lists, and can ask it to prepare stock updates for their own products. The assistant never shows another seller's data.
