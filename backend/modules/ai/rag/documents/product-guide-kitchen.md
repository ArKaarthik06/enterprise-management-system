# Kitchen Product Guide

Advice for choosing and caring for products in the **Kitchen** category.

## Espresso makers
- Choose a machine with at least 15 bar pump pressure for a good crema.
- Use filtered water. Descale every 2 to 3 months, or every month in hard-water areas.
- Clean the portafilter and the steam wand after every use.
- Warranty: 2 years on the machine body, 6 months on seals and gaskets.

## Blenders
- A glass pitcher keeps flavours clean and resists scratches, but it is heavier than plastic. Plastic pitchers are better for homes with children.
- For hot soups, fill the pitcher only up to the marked line and leave the lid vent open a little to release steam.
- Do not run the motor for more than 60 seconds without a rest. Let it cool for 2 minutes between long runs.
- Most pitchers and lids are dishwasher safe, but the base is never to be immersed.

## Stand mixers
- A 4.5 to 5 litre bowl is enough for most families, for example a single large bread loaf or two dozen cookies.
- Use the dough hook on speed 2 only. Higher speeds can overheat the motor.
- Wash attachments by hand if they are aluminium, otherwise they can turn grey in the dishwasher.

## Food safety
- Replace cracked or deeply scratched non-stick surfaces.
- Do not use metal tools on non-stick surfaces.
- Keep electrical bases dry and unplug the appliance before cleaning.

## Choosing by budget
- Under $150: entry blenders and simple kettles.
- $150 to $350: stand mixers and good blenders.
- Above $350: espresso machines with a steam wand and a built-in grinder.
