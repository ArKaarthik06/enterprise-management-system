# Payment and Pricing Policy

## 1. Prices
- All prices on the marketplace are shown in US dollars (USD) and include taxes unless the product page says otherwise.
- The price you see when you place the order is the price you pay. The order stores this as the "price at purchase", so later price changes never affect an existing order.
- Sellers may change prices at any time, but a change never applies to orders that are already confirmed.

## 2. Pricing errors
If a product is listed with an obvious pricing mistake (for example 90% below the usual price), the marketplace may cancel the affected orders and refund in full.

## 3. Payment methods
Accepted: major credit and debit cards, bank transfer and approved digital wallets. We never ask for card numbers, CVV codes or passwords in chat. **Do not type payment card details into the AI assistant.** The assistant automatically hides card numbers it detects, but customers should still never share them.

## 4. Order value limits
- A single product line can have a quantity of at most **10 units** when ordering through the AI assistant.
- An order placed through the AI assistant may not exceed **$5,000** in total. Larger orders must be placed on the checkout page, where extra verification is applied.

## 5. Promotions
Only one promotion code can be used per order. Promotions cannot be combined with price-match offers and have no cash value.

## 6. Invoices
An invoice is available for each confirmed order in the order details page. Business customers can add a tax identifier to their profile and it will appear on future invoices.

## 7. Chargebacks
If a customer disputes a payment with their bank, the related order is placed on hold until the dispute is resolved. Please contact support first, since most problems can be solved faster directly.
