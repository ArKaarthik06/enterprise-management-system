# Order Cancellation Policy

## 1. When an order can be cancelled
A customer can cancel an order by themselves while its status is **pending** or **confirmed**. Orders that are **shipped** or **delivered** cannot be cancelled. For delivered orders, use the Return and Refund Policy instead.

## 2. What happens after cancellation
- The order status changes to "cancelled".
- Reserved stock is returned to the product immediately, so other customers can buy it.
- The full amount including shipping is refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## 3. Cancellation by the marketplace
We may cancel an order, with a full refund, when:
- The product is no longer available or the listed price was a clear error.
- Our fraud checks cannot verify the order.
- The seller account has been suspended.

## 4. Partial cancellation
An order containing several products cannot be partially cancelled. Cancel the whole order and place a new order for the items you still want.

## 5. Cancellation through the AI assistant
The AI assistant can cancel an order on behalf of the logged-in customer. Because cancelling changes data, the assistant always shows a summary first and the customer must press the **Confirm** button before anything happens. The assistant can only cancel the customer's own orders.

## 6. Repeated cancellations
Accounts with more than 5 cancelled orders in 30 days may be reviewed by the trust and safety team and can be temporarily limited from placing new orders.
