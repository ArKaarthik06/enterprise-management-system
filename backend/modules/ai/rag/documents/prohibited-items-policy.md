# Prohibited and Restricted Items Policy

Sellers must not list the items below. Listings that break this policy are removed and repeated violations lead to account suspension.

## 1. Prohibited items
- Weapons, ammunition and explosives.
- Illegal drugs, drug paraphernalia and unapproved medical devices.
- Counterfeit, pirated or stolen goods.
- Hazardous materials such as unmarked chemicals, fireworks and lithium cells sold loose.
- Recalled products listed on a government recall database.
- Personal data, accounts, licence keys or hacked services.

## 2. Restricted items (extra approval from an administrator)
- Electrical appliances without a recognised safety mark (for example CE, UL or BIS).
- Items with built-in batteries above 100 Wh.
- Food and drinks, which need a valid food business registration.
- Items for children under 3 years, which need test certificates.

## 3. Household and kitchen category rules
- Appliances must state the rated voltage and power in the description.
- Knives and blades are allowed in the Kitchen category when they are for cooking only.
- Pressure cookers and gas appliances must include a safety certificate reference.

## 4. How violations are handled
1. First violation: listing removed and seller warned.
2. Second violation within 90 days: listing privileges paused for 14 days.
3. Third violation: account suspended and pending payouts held for investigation.

Administrators can review any listing and set the product status to suspended. Sellers can appeal within 10 days by sending evidence through the seller support page.
